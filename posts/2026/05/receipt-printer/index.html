Receipt Printing Adventures

2026-05-09 · 1 min · jp

This was a quick and fun little project that I cobbled together based on some similar projects I’d seen on Twitter.

The idea is simple: push a button, and a “daily summary” report prints out. It has the date, weather, upcoming rocket launches, stock info, and a daily fact and quote.

I also decided to add a second button that prints out a random picture of my girlfriend and me. (It seemed like a fun little Easter egg!)

Since I already had an old thermal receipt printer with a Raspberry Pi Zero W sticky-taped to the side of it lying around from my bakery order system project, this was very easy to set up.
